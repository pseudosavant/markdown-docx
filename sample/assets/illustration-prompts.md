# Showcase Illustration Prompt Set

All five assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The first image established the cast. Each later prompt used one or more earlier images as visual references.

## Shared visual direction

Use case: illustration-story.

Create crisp black ink editorial comic line art on a clean white background. Use expressive varied line weight, white fills, and sparse hatching. Use no color and no gray wash. Include no text, captions, speech bubbles, logos, watermarks, or panel borders.

Keep exactly three recurring dogs. Pepper is a small wiry black-and-white terrier with upright ears, a spotted muzzle, and a plain round collar. Biscuit is a stocky floppy-eared retriever with a broad friendly face and a plain triangular bandana. Juniper is a tall slim greyhound with long legs, a narrow face, and a simple striped collar.

## dog-run-start.png

Draw the three dogs beginning a cheerful run together on a curving park path just after leaving a garden gate. Pepper leads with alert confidence. Biscuit bounds happily. Juniper runs with elegant patience. Use a wide landscape composition with full bodies, clear silhouettes, sparse park details, and generous white space.

## dog-lunch-chase.png

Draw the same three dogs on the park path as a closed paper lunch bag and loose napkin blow downhill. Biscuit veers toward it with comic delight. Pepper skids and looks back in alarm. Juniper braces and tries to keep the group orderly. Keep the bag several feet ahead. Use a wide landscape composition with motion lines and harmless escalating mischief.

## dog-wagon-rescue.png

Draw the same three dogs as the lunch bag lands in a small empty wagon that begins rolling gently downhill. Biscuit has bumped the wagon and looks surprised. Pepper leaps over one handle. Juniper plants her feet and catches the loose pull rope in her mouth. Add a few flying dry leaves. Keep the action energetic and safe.

## dog-run-finish.png

Draw the same three dogs sitting proudly beside the safely stopped wagon and a park bench. The closed lunch bag is intact on the bench. Pepper has one dry leaf on an ear. Biscuit watches the bag hopefully. Juniper keeps one paw near the wagon rope. Add one friendly adult runner kneeling beside them. Keep the person secondary.

## dog-trio-cameo.png

Draw a tight horizontal portrait of the same three dog heads and upper shoulders. Arrange Pepper, Biscuit, and Juniper from left to right. Give each an amused expression. Use a very wide compact composition with clean silhouettes and no background scenery. The smaller `dog-trio-inline.png` is a layout-ready derivative of this illustration.
